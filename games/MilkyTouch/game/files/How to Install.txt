[How to install]
1. Download the whole "Milky Touch Story Mods by imgfylalbm" folder.
2. Inside, you'll find 8 images folders labeled with mod names, the "ClaudiaAudio" folder, and 10 scripts.
3. Take everything (9 folders & 10 scripts) and put them in the "game" folder.
4. The script files will overwrite the original ones, and the folders should now be in the location "game/ClaudiaAudio", "game/ClaudiaCimages", "game/ClaudiaWimages", and so on.
5. Enjoy.
